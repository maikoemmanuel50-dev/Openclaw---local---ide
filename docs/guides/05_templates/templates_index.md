## Reuse rules

- Templates parameterize brand values: palette (5 chapter LUTs, #FFD54F hero),
  type system (Inter/Nunito Sans), motion standards (3–5f ease, stat hold
  36–48f).
- Apply the same template in future episodes for consistency; adjust only the
  data/narrative content.
- Confirmed deliverable use: TextStat/Fairlight templates are already baked
  into the Resolve assembly and mix.
