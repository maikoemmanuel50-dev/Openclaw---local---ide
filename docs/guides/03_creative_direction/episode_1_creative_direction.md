# Episode 1 Creative Direction ("Africa Season 1 — Silicon Savannah")

The unified creative brief for Episode 1, distilled from the script, scene spec,
style bible, and creative gate. Future episodes reuse this structure.

## The episode

- Format: motion-graphics / illustrated documentary (2.5D parallax).
- Runtime target: exactly 7:00 (420 s) @ 24 fps (~850 words).
- Narration: the user's own recorded voice (`assets/audio/vo/episode_01_vo.wav`
  locks timing). Never substitute an AI voice.
- 10 scenes across 5 narrative chapters (Dawn → Daylight → DarkData →
  CoolTension → HopefulDusk).

## Throughline

Silicon Savannah's leap: mobile money, solar, hubs, and the gap to a
secondary-city future. Hero motif = yellow ball; humanity = ball-head bodies.
**Current waiver (PRODUCTION_STATUS.md): yellow-ball hero removed from renders —
Scene 01 kept as-is; scenes 02–10 render without the ball.** Creative gate says
follow the live status, do not silently reinstate V2 overlays.

## Editorial decisions (unified)

- Kinetic edit 5-track: V1 plates · V2 YB (empty w/ waiver) · V3/V4 B-roll ·
  V5 TextStat. Stats on V5 sync to VO; hold 36–48f.
- Music continuous, ducked under VO; ambient bed keeps the doc grounded.
- Grade: AgX plates, per-chapter soft-pop (5 LUTs).
- End card: brand logo, holds 8 s, Inter 120px.

## Reuse for future episodes

Keep: style bible, aesthetics, palette/LUTs, motion rules, audio mix, delivery
standards. Swap only: script, scene map, spec table, cite the new
`PRODUCTION_STATUS.md` for live waivers.
