# Motion & Editorial Rules

Keep the cut tight, readable, and locked to the narration. Every number below
is a target other episodes reuse.

## Standard numbers

- Entrance/exit ease: 3–5 frames, ease-in/out.
- Stat readability hold: 36–48 frames (min readable).
- Camera drift: 0.3–0.6% scale/frame; subtle.
- Icon stagger: 12 frames (0.5 s) between list items.
- Pulse cycle: 24 frames (1 s). Draw-on speed: 2 frames/segment.
- Kinetic ASL: 0.4–1.0 s (target ~0.7); secondary B-roll 1.0–2.0 s.

## Transitions

- Vocabulary locked: cut (0f), slide wipe (6–10f), fade (6–8f), morph (12–18f).
- No fade-to-black (reads as a stop).
- Permission for hard palette snap at chapter cuts.

## Editing discipline

- Nothing moves without VO covering it; orphan motion is a defect.
- 1 focal idea per frame; avoid more than 2 simultaneous moving labels.
- Background continuity within a scene pair; shift = new chapter.
- Kinetic B-roll: match cutting rhythm to music/VO pulse; varied shot length
  over monotone.

## Blender motion lock

- Keyframes/Geometry Nodes motion locked per `OBJECT_MOTION_LOCK.md` — no
  motion that fights the VO timeline. Re-verify after any asset swap.

## Engagement studies (industry)

Documentary-style animation outperform basic stock + text (+23% engagement in
industry comparisons); short-form/micro-docs 5–10 min are the dominant format
for faceless educational content in 2026.
