# Documentary Aesthetic Lock

The look that separates "explainer clip" from "documentary." Applied to every
scene; the Resolve Color page applies the final polish.

## Hard rules

- **Faceless only.** Yellow ball `#FFD54F` / YB-Body (charcoal torso + ball
  head). No faces. No competing mascots (waived only per `PRODUCTION_STATUS.md`).
- **VO/script drives picture** — no orphan motion.
- **DOF on hero when present**; shallow for intimacy, deep for data/wide shots.
- **Motion blur subtle** — documentary readability > action blur.
- **Final look in Resolve**: Blender AgX Medium-High plates → chapter soft-pop
  polish on the Color page. One grade family per chapter.
- No 4K until PRE_4K_GATE clears.

## Energy / rhythm guardrails

- Chapter snap: hard palette change at chapter cuts.
- Saturation contrast: S07–S08 background desaturate 30–40% while the ball stays
  warm — the S09 flood of gold then feels huge.
- Neon only in the DarkData chapter; never spill neon into Dawn.
- Never: constant camera shake, too many simultaneous labels, rainbow gradients,
  long crossfades.
